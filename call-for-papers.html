CONFERENCE-2027

Call for Papers

We invite original research contributions, extended abstracts, posters, demonstrations, and case studies relevant to fair and explainable AI, data science, and responsible AI research.

Conference Theme

Fairness, Explainability, Responsible AI, and Network Science

Submission Topics

  • Computational Approaches for Fair and Explainable AI
  • Fairness and Explainability in Graph Neural Networks
  • Ethical, Responsible, and Trustworthy AI
  • Behavioral Aspects of Explainable AI
  • Evaluation and Benchmarking of Fair and Explainable AI
  • Applications of Fair and Explainable AI
